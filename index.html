Free, with no feature locks and no in-app purchases in this version.

Highlight, annotate, find later

  • Four colors; add a note to any highlight.
  • Select text to get a floating toolbar, or use the right-click menu.
  • Side panel lists all highlights grouped by page, with a color filter and full-text search (quote, note, page title, folder, tags). Click a highlight to jump back to its spot on the page.

Highlights that survive page changes

  • Each highlight is restored by matching the quoted text plus a short snippet of surrounding context — not character offsets or XPath, which break when a page is edited.
  • If a highlight can no longer be located, it is never silently dropped: it stays in the side panel marked “not found”, and you can re-locate it by selecting the text on the page and clicking Bind.

Import & export

  • Weava CSV import: a preview shows per-page counts plus duplicate and unusable-row tallies before anything is written. Imported highlights re-anchor when you open the page.
  • Export your data at any time to JSON, CSV, or Markdown. Your data is never held hostage.

Private by default

  • No account, no sign-up. There is no server — everything is stored in your browser’s local storage (chrome.storage.local) and nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Limits, stated plainly

  • No PDF highlighting in this version.
  • It cannot run on browser-internal pages (chrome://, edge://), other extensions’ pages, or the extension stores themselves.
  • Re-anchoring is text matching, not magic: heavy rewrites may need a manual Bind.
