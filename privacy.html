Privacy Policy — Highlighter

Effective date: October 5, 2026 · Applies to Highlighter （高亮标注） version 0.2.0 and later 0.x versions.

The short version

Highlighter has no account, no server, and no analytics. Your highlights, notes, and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage. Nothing you highlight, write, or visit is collected, transmitted, sold, or shared — there is nowhere for it to go.

What is stored, and where

When you create a highlight, the extension saves the highlighted text, a short snippet of surrounding context (used to find the spot again), its color, your note, the page URL and title, and timestamps. Imported highlights may additionally keep the folder and tags from the imported file. All of this lives in chrome.storage.local on your own device, inside your browser profile.

What is NOT collected

  • No personal information, no identifiers, no usage statistics, no crash reports.
  • No browsing history is recorded beyond the pages you choose to highlight on (a highlight has to remember which page it belongs to).
  • No cookies, no third-party scripts, no remote requests of any kind. The extension makes zero network calls.

Page content

To render and restore your highlights, the extension's content script reads the text of pages you visit in your browser only, matches it against your stored highlights, and wraps the matching ranges. This processing happens entirely on your device; page content is never copied out, stored separately, or sent anywhere.

Permissions and why each is needed

PermissionPurpose
storageSave highlights, notes, and settings in local browser storage.
activeTabAfter you click an item in the side panel, communicate with the tab you are interacting with (jump to a highlight, manual re-locate).
contextMenusProvide the right-click "Highlight selection" menu entry.
sidePanelDisplay the highlight list in the browser's side panel.
Content script on web pagesRender and restore your own highlights on pages you visit (see "Page content" above).

Import and export

Import reads only a CSV file that you pick with the file chooser. Export downloads a file (JSON, CSV, or Markdown) to your device when you click the export button. Both are local file operations; no data leaves your device.

Deleting your data

Delete individual highlights in the side panel, or remove the extension from your browser — uninstalling deletes all locally stored data with it. There is no server copy to request deletion of, because there is no server.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any of the above (for example, an optional sync feature), this page will be updated before that version ships, and any feature that transmits data will be opt-in and off by default.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support/feedback channel on the extension's store listing page.

隐私政策 — 高亮标注

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 5 日 · 适用于「高亮标注」（Highlighter）0.2.0 及后续 0.x 版本。

一句话版本

本扩展没有账号、没有服务器、没有任何统计。你的高亮、备注与设置只保存在 你浏览器本地存储中。你标注的内容、写下的备注、访问的页面，都不会被收集、传输、出售或 分享——因为根本没有可以传去的地方。

保存什么、存在哪里

创建高亮时，扩展保存：被高亮的文本、其前后一小段上下文（用于再次打开时定位）、颜色、 你的备注、页面 URL 与标题、时间戳。导入的高亮还可能带有导入文件中的文件夹与标签。 以上全部保存在你自己设备上、浏览器配置文件内的 chrome.storage.local 中。

不收集什么

  • 不收集个人信息、标识符、使用统计、崩溃报告，一概不收集。
  • 除你主动标注的页面外，不记录浏览历史（高亮必须记住它属于哪个页面）。
  • 无 Cookie、无第三方脚本、无任何远程请求——扩展不发出任何网络请求。

页面内容如何处理

为渲染与恢复高亮，内容脚本会读取你所访问页面的文本，但仅在你的浏览器内与本地 保存的高亮做匹配并包裹对应区间。处理完全在本地进行；页面内容不会被复制出去、另行存储 或发送到任何地方。

权限及用途

权限用途
storage将高亮、备注与设置保存在浏览器本地存储。
activeTab在你点击侧边栏条目后，与你正在交互的标签页通信（跳转定位、手动重新定位）。
contextMenus提供右键菜单「高亮选中文本」入口。
sidePanel在浏览器侧边栏中展示高亮列表。
页面内容脚本在你访问的页面上渲染与恢复你自己的高亮（见上「页面内容如何处理」）。

导入与导出

导入只读取你通过文件选择器主动选定的 CSV 文件；导出在你点击导出按钮时把文件 （JSON / CSV / Markdown）下载到你的设备。两者都是本地文件操作，数据不离开你的设备。

删除数据

可在侧边栏逐条删除高亮；或直接卸载扩展——卸载即删除全部本地数据。不存在需要申请 删除的服务器副本，因为没有服务器。

政策变更

若未来版本改变以上任何一条（例如增加可选同步功能），本页会在该版本发布前更新， 且任何会传输数据的功能都将默认关闭、需你主动开启。
